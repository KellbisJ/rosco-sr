## Platform

## Users

Primary: homeowners in Perth, WA who are mid-renovation and need an interior space stripped out, gutted, or cleared before the next trade arrives. They want the work done cleanly, quickly, and without their fixtures, floors, or valuables getting wrecked.

## Product Purpose

ROSCO S&R performs professional strip outs, non-structural demolition, removals (kitchen, bathroom, floor, tile), concrete cutting, floor grinding, surface preparation, and rubbish removal across Perth. It exists so a homeowner's renovation can start from a clean, safe, intact site — handled with care instead of brute force.

Success for the site: the homeowner sees real proof of the work, then requests a free quote — via phone, email, or the embedded form.

## Positioning

Careful, precise removal that preserves the homeowner's assets — "removed respectfully and intact" — as opposed to rough demolition crews that treat the site as disposable. The work itself is the proof: a small, hands-on operation whose photos and videos carry the pitch.

## Operating Context

- Jobs happen across Perth, WA; site is the shop window, the gallery is the evidence.
- The business is currently a small entrepreneurship, not a corporate brand — the site must not pretend otherwise.

## Product Principles

1. Careful, asset-preserving removal is the differentiator — the site must make "respectful and intact" visible in the work, not just claimed in copy.
2. Photo and video evidence carries the pitch. No credential claims, no invented scale.
3. The free quote is the goal — phone, email, and form must be reachable from every surface with one action.
4. Homeowners are the audience — plain, Aussie, plainspoken language; never trade jargon or corporate polish that overstates the operation's size.
5. Honest about being a small operation — small is a feature (direct line to the person doing the work), never something to disguise.
